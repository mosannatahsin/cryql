# Research methodology

CryQL follows the GraphQL specification at <https://spec.graphql.org/> and
models standard introspection response shapes. Runtime-specific error
wording is treated as evidence, not as a universal contract.

External browsing MCP research was not available in this implementation run:
Bright Data credentials were rejected and Firecrawl returned HTTP 401. No
unsupported research results or benchmark claims are included.
